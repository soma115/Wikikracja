{% trans "How do activities work?" %}
{% trans "Mine" %}

{% blocktrans %}The tab collects activities that directly concern you: activities that you have undertaken (you are the owner) and activities in which you have declared your help (you have clicked "I want to help").{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Pending" %}

{% blocktrans %}Proposals move to realization automatically when the vote balance reaches 2 and the required team size is met. A coordinator must be assigned and counts as one team member.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "In progress" %}

{% blocktrans %}Activities move automatically to realization once the vote and team requirements have been met. If those requirements stop being met, the activity automatically returns to proposal and keeps its votes and team. Voting remains open, and the activity is automatically rejected when votes against outnumber votes for by at least one.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Completed" %}

{% blocktrans %}Activities closed as completed or cancelled, plus activities automatically rejected when votes against outnumber votes for by at least one. Reopening returns an activity to proposal, resets votes, and keeps the coordinator and approved helpers.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Declaration of aid" %}

{% blocktrans %}The "I want to help" button is a declaration of commitment - not a like. The number of people who clicked it is visible on the activity tile. The coordinator and approved volunteers carry out the activity, not the whole group. Realization starts automatically when the vote balance reaches 2 and the required approved team size is met.{% endblocktrans %}
