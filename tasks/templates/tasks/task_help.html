{% trans "How do activities work?" %}
{% trans "Mine" %}

{% blocktrans %}The tab collects activities that directly concern you: activities that you have undertaken (you are the owner) and activities in which you have declared your help (you have clicked "I want to help").{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Pending" %}

{% blocktrans %}Proposals that have not started realization. Anyone can take coordination. To start realization, the coordinator needs a vote balance of at least 2 and the required number of people in the team; the coordinator counts as one team member.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "In progress" %}

{% blocktrans %}Activities explicitly started by their coordinator after the vote and team requirements have been met. Voting remains open, and a balance of −2 or lower automatically rejects the activity. The coordinator can return it to proposal; this resets votes and clears the team.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Completed" %}

{% blocktrans %}Activities closed as completed or cancelled, plus activities automatically rejected at a vote balance of −2 or lower. Reopening returns an activity to proposal, resets votes, and keeps the coordinator and approved helpers.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Declaration of aid" %}

{% blocktrans %}The "I want to help" button is a declaration of commitment - not a like. The number of people who clicked it is visible on the activity tile. Starting realization requires a vote balance of at least 2 and enough approved team members.{% endblocktrans %}
